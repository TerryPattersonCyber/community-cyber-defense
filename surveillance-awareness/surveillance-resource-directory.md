# Surveillance Resource Directory

A curated directory of government, technical, civil-liberties, research, and community resources related to automated license plate readers, Flock Safety cameras, facial recognition, location tracking, surveillance camera networks, public records, privacy, and community oversight.

This directory is designed to help readers find credible sources after using the Community Cyber Defense Project's surveillance guides and toolkits.

---

# How to Use This Directory

Surveillance research is stronger when multiple types of sources are compared.

Use:

### Government Sources

For laws, agency policies, contracts, public records, official reports, and government decisions.

### Technical Sources

For understanding how technology works, its capabilities, limitations, cybersecurity considerations, and accuracy.

### Vendor Sources

For understanding what a company says its own product does.

### Independent Research

For outside analysis of technology, privacy, civil liberties, security, misuse, and policy.

### Advocacy Organizations

For community organizing, model policies, litigation, legislation, and civic-action resources.

Whenever possible, compare more than one category.

# Automated License Plate Readers

## Electronic Frontier Foundation

### EFF — Automated License Plate Readers

https://sls.eff.org/technologies/automated-license-plate-readers-alprs

EFF's Street-Level Surveillance project provides educational information about automated license plate readers, how ALPR systems work, the information they collect, and the privacy implications of large-scale license plate databases.

Useful for:

- Understanding ALPR technology
- Surveillance policy
- Privacy research
- Community education
- Public-records advocacy

---

## American Civil Liberties Union

### ACLU — Fight Creepy ALPR Cameras / Get the Flock Out

https://www.aclu.org/campaigns-initiatives/get-the-flock-out

The ACLU's current national campaign focuses on automated license plate readers and community opposition to large-scale ALPR networks.

The campaign includes:

- ALPR information
- Community advocacy
- State activity
- Legislative efforts
- Contract opposition
- Local organizing resources

---

### ACLU — How to Fight Deployment of Flock and Other Mass Surveillance License Plate Readers in Your Community

https://www.aclu.org/news/privacy-technology/tracking-alpr-cameras/how-to-fight-deployment-of-flock-and-other-mass-surveillance-license-plate-readers-in-your-community

A detailed community advocacy guide covering:

- Local organizing
- Public meetings
- Contacting elected officials
- Contract review
- Data retention
- Data sharing
- Law-enforcement use
- Policy safeguards
- Legislative advocacy

This is particularly useful for communities considering whether to oppose deployment, expansion, or renewal of an ALPR system.

# Flock Safety

## Flock Safety Trust Center

### Data Privacy & Protection

https://www.flocksafety.com/trust/data-privacy

Flock Safety's own documentation describing its privacy and security practices.

Useful for researching vendor-stated information concerning:

- Data collection
- Retention
- Encryption
- Access controls
- Audit logging
- Data sharing
- Customer control

Because this is vendor documentation, compare it with local agency policies, contracts, public records, and independent sources.
