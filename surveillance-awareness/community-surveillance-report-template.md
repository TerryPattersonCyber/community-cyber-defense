# Community Surveillance Assessment Report Template

A standardized reporting template for publishing evidence-based assessments of local surveillance technologies.

This template is designed for:

- Nonprofits
- Community organizations
- Journalists
- Researchers
- Students
- Privacy advocates
- Cybersecurity professionals
- Civic technology organizations
- Public officials

The goal is to help organizations publish surveillance research in a format that is:

- Clear
- Evidence-based
- Reproducible
- Accessible
- Transparent about uncertainty
- Useful to residents and decision-makers

# Executive Summary

Provide a short plain-language summary of the most important findings.

Recommended length:

**150–300 words**

Include:

- What technology was reviewed
- Which agency operates it
- Why the review was conducted
- Most important verified findings
- Strongest documented safeguards
- Most significant weaknesses or gaps
- Most important unknowns
- Upcoming decisions or deadlines
- Major recommendations

Avoid introducing unsupported conclusions in the executive summary.

# Finding Status Legend

Use consistent labels throughout the report.

## VERIFIED

Supported by reliable primary evidence.

## REPORTED

Supported by credible secondary reporting.

## AGENCY CLAIM

Stated by the agency but not independently verified.

## VENDOR CLAIM

Stated by the vendor.

## ADVOCACY CLAIM

Stated by an advocacy organization.

## ANALYSIS

Conclusion derived from documented evidence.

## UNVERIFIED

Identified but not adequately confirmed.

## UNKNOWN

Reliable information has not been located.
