# Surveillance Research Toolkit

A practical toolkit for researching automated license plate readers, Flock Safety cameras, surveillance camera networks, facial recognition systems, and other surveillance technologies used in a community.

This toolkit is designed to help residents move from general concern to documented research.

The goal is to answer:

**What technology exists?**

**Who controls it?**

**What does it cost?**

**What information does it collect?**

**Who can access the information?**

**How long is the information retained?**

**Who can the information be shared with?**

**What policies and safeguards govern the system?**

**When can the public influence its future?**
