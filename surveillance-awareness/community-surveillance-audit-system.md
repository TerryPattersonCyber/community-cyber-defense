# Community Surveillance Audit System

## An Open Community Surveillance Research & Accountability Framework

The Community Surveillance Audit System (CSAS) is a structured civic-technology framework designed to help communities investigate, document, evaluate, monitor, and report the use of surveillance technologies.

The system connects the educational, research, cybersecurity, policy, and community-action resources of the Community Cyber Defense Project into one repeatable workflow.

It is designed for:

- Residents
- Nonprofits
- Community organizations
- Journalists
- Researchers
- Students
- Educators
- Privacy advocates
- Cybersecurity professionals
- Public officials

The long-term goal is to develop this framework into an interactive web application capable of guiding users through a community surveillance investigation from beginning to end.

---

# The Core Problem

Information about government surveillance systems is often scattered across:

- Government websites
- Contracts
- Procurement portals
- Meeting agendas
- Meeting minutes
- Policies
- Vendor websites
- Transparency portals
- Public-records responses
- Audit logs
- Budgets
- News reports
- Technical documentation

Residents may not know:

- What to search for
- Which documents matter
- Which questions to ask
- How to evaluate safeguards
- How to distinguish claims from evidence
- What records to request
- When contracts expire
- Who controls the decision
- How to turn findings into meaningful action

The Community Surveillance Audit System is designed to organize that process.

↓

↓

How does the program compare with documented policies, prior years, or other jurisdictions?

↓

↓

## 9. ACT

What lawful actions are available?

↓

## 10. TRACK

What future decisions, renewals, changes, and deadlines matter?

↓

## 11. PUBLISH

How can findings be shared responsibly?
