# Fraud Prevention

Practical resources designed to help people recognize scams, protect their personal and financial information, and respond when fraud occurs.

## Available Resources

### 🚨 [Scam & Fraud Defense Guide](scam-and-fraud-defense-guide.md)

A beginner-friendly guide covering:

- Phishing and smishing
- Impersonation scams
- Verification code scams
- Job and employment scams
- Fake check scams
- Romance scams
- Investment and cryptocurrency scams
- Account takeover
- Payment scams
- AI, voice cloning, and deepfake scams
- Fraud response and recovery
- Fraud reporting resources

## Key Principle

**Pause. Verify. Protect.**

Slow down when faced with an unexpected request, independently verify who you are communicating with, and protect your money, accounts, and personal information before taking action.
