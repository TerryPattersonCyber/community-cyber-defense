# Community Cyber Defense Project

## Mission

The Community Cyber Defense Project is a community-centered cybersecurity initiative focused on making digital safety, privacy, fraud prevention, surveillance awareness, and cybersecurity education easier to understand and more accessible.

The goal is to translate cybersecurity concepts into practical resources that people can use in everyday life.

## Why This Project Exists

Cybersecurity threats affect everyone, but access to understandable cybersecurity education and privacy resources is not always equal.

This project is being developed to create practical, accessible resources that help individuals and communities better understand:

- Online privacy
- Identity theft and fraud prevention
- Phishing and social engineering
- Password and account security
- Digital surveillance and data collection
- Device and network security
- Cybersecurity career awareness
- Safe use of emerging technologies

## Current Project Areas

### Digital Safety
Practical guidance for protecting personal accounts, devices, and information.

### Privacy
Resources explaining online tracking, data collection, permissions, and privacy controls.

### Fraud Prevention
Educational resources covering scams, phishing, identity theft, and common fraud techniques.

### Surveillance Awareness
Accessible explanations of technologies such as cameras, license plate readers, facial recognition, location tracking, and other forms of digital surveillance.

### Cybersecurity Education
Beginner-friendly cybersecurity resources designed to make technical concepts easier to understand.
