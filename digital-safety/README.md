# Digital Safety

Practical, beginner-friendly resources designed to help individuals protect their accounts, devices, personal information, and online activity.

## Available Resources

### 🛡️ [Digital Safety Starter Guide](digital-safety-starter-guide.md)

A beginner-friendly introduction to everyday cybersecurity covering:

- Strong and unique passwords
- Multi-factor authentication
- Email security
- Phishing awareness
- Software updates
- Device security
- App permissions
- Public Wi-Fi
- Backups
- Account monitoring
- What to do if an account is compromised

## Coming Next

Future resources will expand on individual digital safety topics with dedicated guides, checklists, and educational materials.
